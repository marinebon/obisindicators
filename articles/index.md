### The obis_h3 store

Build and serve a DuckDB store of OBIS binned to H3, filter it by any
WoRMS taxon or Essential Ocean Variable, and roll it up across
resolutions, decades and places. The store articles are precomputed
against a local store by `data-raw/precompute_articles.R` and double as
the figure notebooks of the OBIS → H3 → EOV manuscript (see `paper/`).
